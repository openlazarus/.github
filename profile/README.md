# OpenLazarus

OpenLazarus builds AI platforms for the companies that keep the world running: people, data, software and AI teammates on one platform the company owns.

Every company gets its own Lazarus: CRM, mail, calendar, messages, meetings, tasks, operations and finance, with AI teammates that work inside it.
